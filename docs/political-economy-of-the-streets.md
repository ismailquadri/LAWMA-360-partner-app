# Street Political Economy: PSP SME Business & Survival Realities

**Target Application:** `LAWMA-360-partner-app` (PSP SME Back-Office Console)  
**Domain Focus:** AWAMN Union Politics, Diesel Supply Chains, and Municipal Revenue Clashes

---

## 1. AWAMN Institutional Politics
- **The Power Structure:** The Association of Waste Managers of Nigeria (AWAMN) is a formidable political bloc representing 400+ business owners with thousands of employees.
- **The Political Memory:** AWAMN defeated the 2017 private concession through legal challenges, legislative lobbying, and operational strikes. Any digital system must be designed as a partnership tool that strengthens AWAMN's institutional viability rather than an external threat.

## 2. The Informal Diesel Market
- **Depot Procurement:** Most PSPs do not buy fuel from major branded filling stations; they negotiate bulk truckload deliveries or buy from local independent marketers at cash discounts.
- **Fuel Quality Risks:** Low-quality adulterated diesel frequently damages fuel injection pumps, causing unexpected fleet grounding. The Partner app's maintenance tracker helps operators isolate fuel-quality pump failures across their vehicle rosters.

## 3. Local Government Revenue Clashes (Conference 57)
- **The Double-Taxation Trap:** Local Government environmental revenue officers frequently accost PSP compactor trucks on municipal roads, demanding local "carriage permits" or council sanitation levies.
- **System Solution:** The platform integrates State-LGA clearance barcodes, demonstrating that 5% of regulatory clearinghouse revenues are remitted to host LGAs, legally shielding the PSP fleet from council impoundment.
