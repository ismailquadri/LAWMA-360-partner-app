# PSP Operator & AWAMN Critiques

**Target Application:** `LAWMA-360-partner-app` (PSP SME Back-Office Console)  
**Audience:** Back-Office Developers, Fintech Engineers, Fleet Product Managers

Existential anxieties, financial concerns, and institutional critiques raised by AWAMN executives and PSP SME business owners:

---

### Critique 1: The 'Visionscape Trauma' & Centralized Collection Terror
> *"In 2017, the Lagos State Government sidelined over 350 local PSP operators who invested their life savings in trucks, brought in a foreign consortium (Visionscape), and centralized all waste billing under PUMAU. The government withheld our revenue, and the entire city drowned in garbage. How do we know this SmartWaste360 platform isn't just another digital trick to take our customer base and hand our routes to a preferred political company?"*  
— **AWAMN State Executive Member**

### Critique 2: Delayed Escrow Payouts & Operational Starvation
> *"Waste management runs on daily cash. Every morning at 5:00 AM, our drivers need cash for diesel, loader stipends, and hydraulic oil. If resident money sits in a government-controlled escrow account and your Friday 5:00 PM transfer fails or gets delayed by bureaucratic approvals, our trucks cannot move on Monday morning. Can you guarantee automated, unblockable bank remittances?"*  
— **PSP Managing Director, Alimosho Zone**

### Critique 3: Inflation vs Capped Household Tariffs
> *"When we were assigned our Alimosho ward in 2021, diesel was ₦280/liter and a front tire was ₦65,000. Today, diesel is ₦1,350/liter and a tire is ₦320,000, but LAWMA still caps our monthly tenement tariff at ₦1,500. How can software help us survive when our operating costs have inflated by 400%?"*  
— **Managing Director, Waste Master Logistics**

### Critique 4: Route Pirating & Cartel Theft
> *"While our compactor is struggling through traffic on Ikotun road, informal cart pushers and illegal private trucks sneak into our estates at night, collect cash from residents, and dump the trash in the nearest canal. How does your system stop these thieves from poaching our revenue?"*  
— **PSP Route Dispatcher, Egbe/Idimu**

### Critique 5: The Commercial Bank Lending Wall
> *"We desperately need to buy new compactor trucks, but commercial banks treat waste management like a criminal enterprise. They ask for audited financials, land collateral in Ikoyi, and 30% interest rates. How will your console help an ordinary PSP SME actually secure a ₦60M asset loan from Sterling Bank or the Bank of Industry?"*  
— **PSP Financial Controller, Lagos West**
